1 · What does a box mean?

Each box represents one orbital. Each arrow represents an electron, with its direction indicating spin. An orbital holds at most two electrons, with opposite spins.

2 · Three rules for placing electrons

Aufbau principle

Fill the lowest available energy orbitals first. A useful order for neutral atoms is 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s → 3d → 4p. Actual orbital energies can change with electron occupancy.

Pauli exclusion principle

Two electrons in the same orbital must have opposite spins: ↑↓, not ↑↑.

Hund’s rule

In orbitals of equal energy within a sublevel, place one electron in each orbital with parallel spins before pairing.

6 · IB-style reasoning questions

1. Explain why nitrogen's three 2p electrons occupy three different orbitals with parallel spins.

Hund's rule: electrons occupy degenerate orbitals singly with parallel spins before pairing, giving a lower-energy arrangement.
2. Explain why the two electrons in the 1s orbital of helium have opposite spins.

The Pauli exclusion principle prevents two electrons in an atom from having the same set of four quantum numbers. Electrons sharing an orbital must therefore have opposite spins.
3. Write the electron configuration of Fe²⁺ and explain which electrons are removed first.

Fe: [Ar] 4s² 3d⁶; Fe²⁺: [Ar] 3d⁶. When a transition metal forms a cation, its 4s electrons are removed before its 3d electrons.
4. Why does a box diagram for 2p⁴ contain one paired orbital and two singly occupied orbitals?

The first three electrons occupy separate equal-energy p orbitals with parallel spins, following Hund's rule. The fourth electron pairs with one of them, with opposite spin to satisfy Pauli's principle.

7 · Exit ticket

Before leaving, explain in one or two sentences the difference between electron configuration and orbital notation. Draw the orbital diagram for phosphorus (P, Z = 15) and identify its unpaired electrons.

Electron configuration lists the number of electrons in each sublevel (P: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p³). Orbital notation shows which individual orbitals are occupied and their electron spins. Phosphorus has three unpaired 3p electrons.
